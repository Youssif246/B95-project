Artificial Turf Photosynthesis Lighting Units

Al-Shaab International Stadium · Baghdad, Republic of Iraq

Deployment Milestone Iraq's First Turf Lighting System
AL-SHAAB & KARBALA INTERNATIONAL STADIUMS // FIRST OF ITS KIND IN IRAQ
The Engineering Challenge & Turnkey Solution

The executive management of Al-Emad Al-Athmal Company visited the headquarters of B95 Project Company in Istanbul to commission custom artificial lighting units for sports stadium turf—marking the first engineering project of its kind in Iraq.

Natural stadium pitch turf requires intense, ground-level photosynthetic radiation during winter months and in deeply shaded zones under grandstand cantilever roofs where natural sunlight is insufficient. B95 Project engineered mobile gantry units that supply balanced Photosynthetically Active Radiation (PAR) directly to the grass blades.

Engineered exclusively with lightweight marine-grade aluminum trusses to eliminate ground compaction, each 12-meter unit is powered by 9 European-origin PHILIPS LED luminaires. The turnkey delivery and operational performance was officially praised by the Iraqi Ministry of Youth and Sports.

12.0m
Ultra-Lightweight Marine Aluminum Gantry

Engineered aluminum structure designed to span 12 meters, minimize chassis weight, and prevent physical damage or root shearing to natural turf.

European-Origin PHILIPS Horticultural LEDs

High-output specialized optoelectronics acting as a complete substitute for natural sunlight during winter and in covered shade areas.
