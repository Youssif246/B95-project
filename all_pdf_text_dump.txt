PROJECT RANGE
ARTIFICIAL LIGHTING UNITS 
FOR SPORTS STADIUM TURF
The management of Al-Emad Al-Athmal Company, represented by Mr. Mustafa, 
visited the headquarters of B95 Project Company in July 2023 to discuss the devel-
opment of artificial lighting units for sports stadium turf. This project marks the first 
of its kind in Iraq, with the units being manufactured and supplied by our company. 
The successful completion of this work was also praised by the Iraqi Ministry of Youth 
and Sports.
About artificial grass lighting units:
These units are used to enhance the growth of stadium turf by providing strong, 
ground-level lighting, acting as a substitute for sunlight during winter and in shaded 
areas where natural light is insufficient.
Technical specifications:
Length: 12 meters
Structure: Made of aluminum to reduce weight and prevent damage to the grass
Lighting: Equipped with 9 European-origin PHILIPS LED lights

PROJECT RANGE
INTERNATIONAL BASRA
AIRPORT RENOVATION PROJECT
Renovation and Rehabilitation Project of Internal Lines Building | Iraq - Basra Interna-
tional Airport
The project aims to rehabilitate and modernize the internal lines building at Basra 
International Airport. The work was carried out in three phases: an engineering 
survey and plan preparation, redesigning according to modern standards inspired 
by Basra’s cultural heritage, and the installation and finishing process.
The project was executed in collaboration with a specialized Turkish engineering 
team, emphasizing sustainability and high-quality materials to create a modern, 
efficient environment that meets the needs of travelers and enhances the overall 
efficiency of the airport.
